Privacy Policy

Information about data used when you use OmniWorldRadio.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Developer and contact

OmniWorldRadio is developed by Laurent Perrin. For privacy questions, contact us using the email address below.

2. Data stored by the app

To provide its features, OmniWorldRadio may store on your device favorite radio stations, custom stations, followed shows, downloaded episodes, playback positions, playlists and collections, history, and certain preferences. This information is used to personalize your experience.

3. iCloud synchronization

When iCloud syncing is enabled and available, certain library data and preferences are synchronized through Apple’s iCloud services between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Downloaded audio files are stored locally and are not transferred by this sync feature.

4. External services and network requests

The app contacts external services to discover radio stations, artwork, podcasts and music metadata, including Radio Browser, Apple’s podcast search services, gPodder and, depending on the content, other directories or streaming providers. During such requests, these providers may receive technical connection data such as an IP address and apply their own privacy policies. Audio streams are delivered by the relevant station or hosting provider.

5. Apple Music, CarPlay and AirPlay

When you open a track in Apple Music or use system features such as CarPlay and AirPlay, their operation is also subject to Apple’s and the selected services’ settings and policies.

6. Email and support requests

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can manage or delete data within the app, including favorites and downloads. Removing the app normally removes local app data, subject to system backup mechanisms. Data previously synchronized with iCloud may require separate actions in your Apple Account settings.

8. Policy updates

This page may be updated if OmniWorldRadio’s functionality or external services change. The last updated date is shown above.
