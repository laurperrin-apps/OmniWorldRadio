OmniWorldRadio Support

Questions, an unavailable podcast, a radio station that does not play, or trouble with syncing? We are here to help.

Contact support

Please describe the issue and, if possible, include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, OmniWorldRadio version, and the name of the affected station or podcast.

OmniWorldRadio@icloud.com

Frequently asked questions

Why will a radio station not play?

Stations are provided by external sources. A stream address can change or become temporarily unavailable. Try another station and report the issue.

Why are some podcast episodes unavailable?

Publishers differ in how they provide RSS feeds, archives and audio files. OmniWorldRadio attempts to find accessible sources, but some episodes are not publicly available.

How can I listen offline?

Download the episode inside the app before traveling. It will then be available in Podcasts → Downloads, even in airplane mode.

How do I sync data to another device?

Enable iCloud on devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Allow time for compatible OmniWorldRadio data to sync. Downloaded audio files remain stored on each device.

How can I change the language?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Apps → OmniWorldRadio → Language, then choose French or English.

CarPlay does not show the right content. What can I do?

Check the CarPlay connection, restart playback from your iPhone and confirm the app is up to date.
